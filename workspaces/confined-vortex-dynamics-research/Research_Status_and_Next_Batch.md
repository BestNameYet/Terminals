## Closest scientific inferences after original-text review

1. A **pressure-gradient sign** requires a declared streamwise coordinate and static pressure stations. The radial pressure distribution of a swirling chamber is not the same as the diffuser's adverse streamwise gradient.
2. **Oscillation mechanisms are topology-dependent**: two return feedback channels and attachment switching (F09), single captive vortex pocket delay (F05), two coaxial ports and acoustic compliance (F06), load-switched gas-return circuit (F02), and nonoscillating diffuser separation (R01) have nonidentical source variables.
3. A **measured switching-frequency change** is not by itself vortex breakdown; outlet throttling, wall attachment, feedback path and source-flow history are competing causes. Distinguish pressure-wave simulation from directly measured feedback pressure.
4. **Negative states** are first-class: high hydraulic load can defeat useful aerator gas reversal; diffuser partition can suppress separation; switching regimes may be hysteretic; certain nozzle–chamber length combinations can suppress acoustic generation.
5. Frequencies in five different geometries cannot be compared directly without their source-defined St/Re/Te and pressure/flow/acoustic boundary states.
